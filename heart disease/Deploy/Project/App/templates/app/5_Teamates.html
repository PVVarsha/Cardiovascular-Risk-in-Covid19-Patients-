HIGH RISK LEVEL
Immediate Attention Required - High Chance of Heart Attack
Category Details
Critical Risk Factors
  • Severe Coronary Artery Disease: Significantly narrowed arteries severely restricting blood flow.
  • Previous Heart Damage: History of major heart attacks or cardiac events.
  • Advanced Cardiomyopathy: Severe heart muscle disease requiring immediate intervention.
  • Critical Valve Disease: Severe valve defects causing significant strain.
Emergency Actions Required
  • Immediate Medical Consultation: Seek emergency cardiac care immediately.
  • Medication Adherence: Strict compliance with all prescribed cardiac medications.
  • Lifestyle Overhaul: Complete transformation of diet and activity levels.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Regular ECG, blood pressure, and cholesterol checks.
  • Emergency Plan: Have a clear emergency response plan for cardiac events.
Intensive Management
  • Advanced Medications: Multiple cardiac medications under specialist supervision.
  • Surgical Evaluation: Consideration for bypass surgery or stents.
  • Strict Diet Control: Very low sodium, low fat, cardiac-specific diet.
  • Supervised Exercise: Only under cardiac rehabilitation supervision.
  • Daily Symptom Tracking: Monitor for chest pain, shortness of breath, swelling.
{% elif prediction_text == 'Medium Risk' %}
MEDIUM RISK LEVEL
Moderate Risk - Requires Careful Management
Category Details
Moderate Risk Factors
  • Moderate Coronary Artery Disease: Some arterial narrowing affecting blood flow.
  • Minor Heart Events: History of minor cardiac issues.
  • Early Cardiomyopathy: Developing heart muscle concerns.
  • Moderate Valve Issues: Valve problems requiring monitoring.
Prevention and Management
  • Regular Medical Follow-ups: Quarterly check-ups with cardiologist.
  • Medication Management: Consistent use of prescribed medications.
  • Lifestyle Modifications: Significant improvements in diet and exercise.
  • Risk Factor Control: Manage blood pressure, cholesterol, and diabetes.
  • Stress Management: Implement regular stress-reduction techniques.
Moderate Exercise Program
  • Brisk Walking: 30 minutes daily, 5 days a week.
  • Cycling: Stationary or outdoor cycling 3-4 times weekly.
  • Swimming: Low-impact water exercises 2-3 times weekly.
  • Light Strength Training: Under professional guidance.
  • Regular Monitoring: Track heart rate and symptoms during exercise.
